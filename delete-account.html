Delete your account and data

App: Homtory · Developer: Vinay Upadhyay · Last updated: 12 August 2026

In short: Homtory keeps your health data on your phone. You can erase it yourself from inside the app in a few taps. If you also turned on the optional cloud backup, you can have your account and that backup permanently deleted, either in the app or by emailing us.

Option 1 — Delete the data on your device (no account needed)

Most people use Homtory without an account, so all of your data lives only on your phone. To erase it:

  1. Open Homtory and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Withdraw Consent, then Wipe All Local Data.
  3. Confirm. This permanently removes all medicines, dose logs, water, fasting, weight, BMI and profile details from the device.

Uninstalling the app also removes all on-device data.

Option 2 — Delete your account and cloud backup

This applies only if you signed in (phone, email, or Google/Microsoft) and used the optional Back up now feature. To permanently delete your account identity and any cloud backup stored under it:

In the app: Settings → Withdraw Consent → Wipe All Local Data, then sign out. To also remove the sign-in identity and any cloud backup, send the request below.

By email (fastest for full account + backup removal): email us from the address or phone number you signed in with, so we can locate your account:

Email a deletion request

We verify the request and delete your account and any associated cloud backup within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in identity (email/phone), and all health data held in the optional cloud backup (medicines, dose logs, water intake, fasting sessions, weight, BMI, profile). On-device data is deleted by the in-app wipe or by uninstalling.
  • Kept: nothing after deletion completes. Homtory is offline-first and runs no analytics, so there is no separate profile or activity history retained about you.
  • Retention: once verified, deletion is permanent with no additional retention period. Backups you never created were never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete in that case.
